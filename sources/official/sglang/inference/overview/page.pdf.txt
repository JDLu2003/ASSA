SGLang powers large-scale production deployments, generating trillions of tokens each day across
more than 400,000 GPUs worldwide. It is hosted under the non-profit open-source organization
LMSYS.




Get Started
SGLang is an inference framework meant for production level serving. It is designed to deliver low-
latency and high-throughput inference across a wide range of setups, from a single GPU to large
distributed clusters.
